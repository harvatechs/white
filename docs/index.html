The Philosophy

The lost art of pure inquiry.

Somewhere along the way, search engines stopped being tools for knowledge and became machines for attention extraction. We built WHITE to return to the original promise of the web.

📄

Clean as Paper

WHITE stands for clean, pristine, and unobstructed. White as a blank page. No ad banners, no sponsored top slots, and no algorithmic casino designed to hijack your focus. Just pure, uncompromised answers.

🛡️

Zero Surveillance

No tracking cookies. No device fingerprinting. No behavioral profiles sold to brokers. Session state is keyed to an anonymous cryptographic token held exclusively in your own browser storage.

⚖️

Of the People

Shape your own internet. With Kagi-inspired domain ranking, you can boost quality blogs (+), lower content farms (-), or purge clickbait domains forever (x). The algorithm belongs to you.

Markov Intelligence

Autocomplete without surveillance.

Traditional engines send every keystroke you type into centralized surveillance logs. WHITE generates query suggestions through local Markov state graphs on your own device.
